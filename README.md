# 💧 Water — a friendly drink water reminder

A tiny water tracker that reminds you to drink. Install it on your phone, allow notifications once,
and it nudges you through the day while a little cartoon drop fills up as you drink.

**Open the app →** https://e0raj.github.io/drink-water-reminder/

No account, no ads, no server. Everything stays on your device and it works fully offline.

---

## Install it on your phone

Installing takes about ten seconds and gives you a real app icon on your home screen.

### Android (Chrome, Edge, Brave)

1. Open the link above in Chrome.
2. Tap the blue **Install** button at the top of the page.
   *(Don't see it? Tap the ⋮ menu → **Install app** / **Add to Home screen**.)*
3. Confirm **Install**. The drop icon appears on your home screen.

### iPhone / iPad (Safari)

1. Open the link above in **Safari** (not Chrome — iPhone only allows installing from Safari).
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow) at the bottom of the screen.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. The drop icon appears on your home screen.

> On iPhone, notifications only work after the app is added to the home screen. Open it from the
> home screen icon, not from Safari.

## Use it

1. **Allow notifications** — the first time you open the app, tap *Allow notifications* and confirm.
   That's the only setup step.
2. **Drink, then tap** — every time you finish a glass, tap **Add a glass**. The drop and the ring grow.
   Use **100 ml** or **500 ml** for a sip or a bottle, and **Undo** if you tapped by mistake.
3. **Change the rhythm** — open **Settings** (the gear at the top right) to pick how often you want to
   be reminded (30 minutes to 2 hours), the hours you're awake, your daily goal and your glass size.
4. **Come back tomorrow** — your history builds up automatically. A flame chip appears once you've hit
   your goal a few days in a row.

You can also log a glass straight from the notification: tap **Drank it**, or **Snooze 10 min** to be
nudged again shortly.

## What's inside

- A cartoon water drop in a blue progress ring that fills up as you drink.
- Reminders every 30 min – 2 hours, only inside the hours you choose.
- Daily goal (1.5 – 3 L), glass size (200 – 500 ml), streak chip, 7-day chart and daily average.
- Vibration and a soft chime, both switchable.
- Works offline, and follows your phone's light or dark theme.

## A note on reminders

Reminders fire reliably while the app is open, including in the background. If you close it
completely, Chrome on Android can still wake it every ~15 minutes once installed. iPhones don't allow
web apps to wake in the background — keep the app open in the background, or reopen it once a day and
any missed reminder shows up straight away.

## Privacy

Your settings and your water history live in your browser's storage on your own device. Nothing is
uploaded anywhere. Clearing the site data (or *Settings → Data → Clear*) resets the app.
